# RUMA Constitution

1. Family is the center of RUMA.
2. PostgreSQL is the business source of truth.
3. AI is never the source of truth.
4. Automation should reduce household cognitive load.
5. Prefer simplicity over premature complexity.
6. Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
7. Every significant architectural change requires an ADR.
8. Documentation must remain consistent with code.
9. Security and tenant isolation are non-negotiable.
10. Build for five years, not only for the current sprint.
11. Challenge requirements respectfully when a better design exists.
12. Developer experience is a product feature.
