# RUMA Brand Foundation

## Name

RUMA

## Category

AI-powered Household Operating System

## Primary Tagline

Your family's second brain.

## Category Descriptor

AI-powered Household Operating System.

## Brand Personality

Premium · Warm · Human · Intelligent · Calm · Modern

## Positioning

RUMA is the shared operating system for a household: one calm place for family coordination, home management, money, assets, knowledge, and AI-powered insights.

## Visual Direction

RUMA uses a premium modern family OS aesthetic:
Soft UI Evolution foundation + Minimalism & Swiss structure + selective Bento Grid + selective AI-Native and Organic influences.

The product should feel premium like Apple/Notion/Linear, but warmer and more human.
